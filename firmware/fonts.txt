# The fonts our firmware image carries beyond the sign's own fonte01..04 (firmware/stock_fonts.txt): one per line, the font's name on the sign and
# the vendored pixel font it is made from (third_party/fonts; licences in THIRD-PARTY.md; the rest of the line, so
# a path may have spaces). They are in our own format
# (.mqf, studio/marquee_studio/mqf.py), loaded by name by our firmware (firmware/ext/fonts.c) after the sign's own,
# in name order (fonts.lst). Picked for a 16 px panel:
# full-height fonts, and smaller ones for two lines or long text. The image build converts them
# (studio/marquee_studio/font_import.py) and adds them, and fonts.lst, to the boot flash; `font_import.py --set` puts the same fonts in
# the studio's content folder, so its pickers and previews show what the sign has.
